01

Who we are

Two audiences, one face: a development finance officer reading a quarterly report, and a business owner deciding whether to walk into the centre.

The organisation supports three kinds of relationship at once — incubatees in full incubation, resellers trading through the LEEMA network, and strategic partners. That structure is the reason the brand speaks to both audiences without changing its voice.

LEEMA Incubation NPC is the parent, and it is the name used in registration, banking, contracts, funder correspondence and PFMA compliance statements. Leema Township Incubator is the programme, and it is the name used in everything public-facing: this website, intake, training, certificates and community materials.

The centre is at Stand 43, Factory A, Bodirelo Industrial Park, Mogwase. The door is open to owners in the North West, Gauteng and the Northern Cape.

Counting note

Projections are written as projections

A projected job is not a created job, and the two are never added together. Where a figure has not been verified against the register, this site shows the slot and names the number required rather than filling it with an estimate.

05

How we write

Grounded and practical. Plain language for township entrepreneurs, precise enough for a development finance officer. Type a sentence and the house rules read it back.

Draft 0 characters

The rules it is reading

Say the thing, then the number. "Ten new jobs projected once the resort is operating" beats "significant employment impact".

Name the enterprise. Reve P Catering, Moela Energies, Thakaramo Security Solution and Training. Never "one of our incubatees".

We for the incubator, you for the entrepreneur, the business's own name in the third person. Never "our clients".

Keep the funder's vocabulary intact. Tranche, business plan, milestone, compliance, PFMA, market access, funding readiness, working capital. These are the terms the money is approved against; do not soften them.

State barriers plainly. Three quarters of the cohort names funding and market access. That gets printed, not hidden.
